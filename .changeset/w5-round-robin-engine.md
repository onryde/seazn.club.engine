W5 round robin (format matrix): an order-free line key, the late-entry catch-up set, pools seated by membership with a floor of two, withdrawal decisions that report their counts, best-Nth across unequal pools always normalised, a cross-pool first-round draw with no pool-mates, frozen Qualified / Out markers on a finished table, and reasoned points adjustments. Design of record: onryde/seazn.club `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-format-matrix-w5-round-robin-design.md` (rulings 276–291); the rules are in `rules/cross-sport.md`.

- BREAKING: `QualStatus` gains `{ kind: "qualified" }`. An exhaustive switch over it needs the new member.
- BREAKING: `qualificationStatus` on a complete stage no longer returns null when it has an order source. It reads frozen markers (X-QS-1): Qualified / Out from `QualificationInput.qualified` (who actually went through), else from the first `cut` active ids of `QualificationInput.order`. Both fields are new and optional. No order source, a malformed one, or a `qualified` that names no active row still gives null. A departed row stays null.
- BREAKING: best-Nth no longer refuses unequal pools, and ignores `normaliseUnequalPools` (ruling 286). Every comparison across unequal pools drops each candidate's results against its pool's bottom k entrants (k = the pool's effective size minus the smallest supplying pool's, ruling 280); equal pools drop nothing. The normalised row is the full row minus the dropped results, so carried openings, sit-out awards and adjustments stay in. `normaliseUnequalPools` is `@deprecated` on `TakeRule` and `SlotDescriptor`: accepted, ignored, kept so stored progressions parse.
- BREAKING: best-Nth over unequal pools needs `PoolTable.results`. A larger pool with none is STAGE_NOT_READY `pool_results_missing` (`pool`). A caller that passed no results and set the flag used to resolve on the raw rows.
- BREAKING: a caller passing results over EQUAL pools with `normaliseUnequalPools: true` stops dropping a result.
- BREAKING: a pool short of nth rows supplies no best-Nth candidate instead of refusing the comparison (RB5-U7); the minimum is taken over the supplying pools only. No supplying pool at all is STAGE_NOT_READY `best_nth_no_supplier` (`rank`, `available`).
- BREAKING: `completeTableStage` with `TableStage.pools` (new: the seating, entrant → pool) seats each pool by membership, so a seated entrant with nothing played is a row. It refuses, each CONFIG_INVALID by name: a pool under two (`pool_too_small`, with `suggestedPools`), invalid rank locks (`rank_lock_out_of_range`, `rank_lock_duplicate_in_pool`, `rank_lock_unknown_entrant`), invalid adjustments (`adjustment_*`, below), a seated entrant the stage does not declare (`entrant_undeclared`), a seated entrant in another pool's result (`fixture_pool_mismatch`), and a result or award naming a declared entrant that is not seated (`entrant_unseated`, only when a result names the entrant; a waiting arrival with no result is not a row and not refused). Without `pools`, rank locks are validated per pool too: a lock outside its pool's range is a named refusal where `applyRankLocks` used to throw unnamed.
- BREAKING: `SEEDING_BESTNTH_UNEQUAL_POOLS` is deprecated: no longer thrown (ruling 286). It stays in the error-code union so a client switching on it still compiles.
- A withdrawal decision ignores a voided fixture even when the caller hands it in `played` with its result (RB5-W4); a walkover counts as played.
- Adjustments rank in the sport's unit: the first of `points` and `wins` in the pool's cascade (`adjustmentUnitOf`). Where wins rank first (badminton, tennis, volleyball) an adjustment of N is N matches' worth on wins; otherwise points. `AdjustmentNote.unit` records it.
- `crossGroupRulesOf` forwards the stage's `gamePoints`, so a reduced best-Nth row recomputes its Sonneborn-Berger in game points without a second wire. `CrossGroupRules.gamePoints` and `SourceTables.gamePoints` (an override) are new and optional. A reduced row holding `sberger` with no game-points rule is CONFIG_INVALID `game_points_missing`.
- `ResolvedProgressionEntry` gains `pool` (the source pool of a best-Nth or group-rank qualifier) and `basis: ComparisonBasis` (`nth`, `matchesCounted` = the largest `played` among the candidates, carried openings and awards included, `dropped`, `levelWith`). `tieUnbroken` on a best-Nth entry now also covers a candidate the cross-group order left level; `basis.levelWith` names the cross-pool part.
- New: `roundRobinLineKey(a, b, leg)`: the order-free key of a round-robin line. It refuses a self-pair, a leg that is not an integer of at least 1, and an empty id or one holding its `:` separator (`line_key_invalid`).
- New: `lateEntryFixtures({ members, newcomer, legs, lines, byes, lastRoundNo })` (`ExistingLine`, `ByeSlot`, `CatchUpPlacement`, `CatchUpFixture`, `CatchUpResult`): the catch-up set for an entrant who joins a started round robin. A future bye holder's unstarted bye slot takes their fixture; the rest are appended as catch-up rounds after `lastRoundNo`. Order: leg first, then the earliest round the opponent played, then listed (seed) order. Within a leg the newcomer's sides alternate, and each pair's side flips between legs. Named refusals: `newcomer_is_member`, `legs_invalid`, `last_round_invalid`, `last_round_below_lines`, `nothing_to_add`.
- New: `crossPoolDraw(seeded, shape, drawSeed)` (`PooledSeed`, `FirstRoundShape`, `CrossPoolDraw`): a first-round draw for `single_elim`, `stepladder` or `page_playoff` that keeps pool-mates apart, deterministic from the STORED draw seed. Named refusals: `no_qualifiers`, `tier_invalid`, `tiers_out_of_order`, `duplicate_entrant`, `tie_link_invalid`, `draw_seed_invalid`, `page_playoff_needs_four`, `pool_separation_impossible`.
- New: `validatePoolSizes(pools, min = 2)` (`pool_too_small`, `suggestedPools`), `validateRankLocks(locks, entrants, pools?)` and `validatePointsAdjustments(adjustments, entrants)` (`adjustment_not_finite`, `adjustment_zero`, `adjustment_reason_missing`, `adjustment_unknown_entrant`): the write-time checks `completeTableStage` runs, for the app to call before it stores.
- New: `TableStage.adjustments` (`PointsAdjustment`: `entrantId`, `delta`, `reason`, `at`) fold into the pool's table before the cascade, and count toward its counted fixtures. A seated stage refuses an adjustment for an unseated entrant (`adjustment_unseated`). `PoolTable.notes` carries one `AdjustmentNote` per adjustment.
- New: `PoolTable.expunged`: entrants a withdrawal expunged. An expunged entrant is never a best-Nth candidate and counts in no pool's effective size. One that the pool has no row for, or that its results still name, is CONFIG_INVALID (`expunged_not_in_pool`, `expunged_has_results`).
- New: the `entrant_withdrawn` `DivisionEvent` carries `played` and `scheduled` under `void_remaining`: the counts the 50 percent decision used (X-WDT-4).
- Stryker: `src/scheduling/catch-up.ts` joins leg `draws-1`.
- Rules: X-RR-1..5, X-CU-1..5, X-PO-1..3, X-WDT-1..4, X-WDT-6, X-UP-1..5, X-PD-1..7, X-QS-1, X-ADJ-1..3 proved: 34 rows, with their `AWAITING_PROOF` entries removed. The W5 app rows still await proof (37 "W5 app PR A", 3 "W5 app PR B").
